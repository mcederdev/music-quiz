Privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

This policy covers Track Night, the music quiz web app and the planned iPhone app, and this website. Track Night is made by an independent developer, reachable at mcederdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in, and we don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • No ads and no analytics. Track Night contains no advertising SDKs (such as AdMob), no analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies.
  • Your quizzes stay in your browser. They are saved on your own device, not on our server.
  • Games are temporary. What players type during a game is kept in the game server's memory only, and is deleted when the game ends.
  • No Spotify connection. Track Night never connects to your Spotify or any other music account.
  • We don't sell or share your data, and nothing is used for advertising.

1. Information stored on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember things between visits. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us, except where a game needs it as described in section 2.

WhatWhy
Your quiz library: quiz names, categories, songs, questions, answer keys, settings and any pictures you add to a quizSo the quiz master can come back to their quizzes
The nickname you last used as a playerTo fill it in for you next time
A random player IDSo you rejoin your team if your phone loses connection. It is generated on your device and isn't linked to who you are.
A random host key for each game you startSo only your browser can run the game as quiz master

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the site data for Track Night in your browser settings. Quiz files you choose to download are ordinary files on your computer that you control.

Share links. When you share a quiz by link, the whole quiz (including its answers) is packed into the link itself, after the "#". That part of a link is never sent to our server; the quiz only reaches whoever you send the link to, and their browser saves it in their quizzes. Anyone with the link can see the quiz, so share it like you would the quiz file.

2. Information used during a game

When a quiz master starts a game, the quiz is sent to the Track Night game server so the game can run. While the game is on, the server holds:

  • the quiz, including songs, questions, pictures and the answer key. The answer key is never sent to players' phones before answers are checked, and a picture is only shown once the game reaches its question;
  • the nickname and team name each player types, and their random player ID;
  • each team's answers, marks and scores.

This is kept in the server's memory only. It is not written to a database or to disk, and it is deleted when the game has been inactive for 12 hours or the server restarts, whichever comes first. Everyone in the same game sees the nicknames, team names and scores, and during peer review a team sees the answers of the team it is marking. Please choose a nickname you're happy for the room to see.

Pictures in a quiz. A picture the quiz master uploads is shrunk in their browser and saved with the quiz. During a game the server shows it to the players in that game. If the quiz master adds a picture by pasting a link to another website instead, players' phones load the picture from that website, which can see their IP address like any website they visit. Only add pictures you have the right to use.

Like any website, the company that hosts the game server may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and to keep the service running. We don't use these logs to identify or profile you.

3. Song year lookups (MusicBrainz)

When a quiz master asks Track Night to look up a song's release year, our server sends that song's artist and title to MusicBrainz, an open music database. The request comes from our server, not your device, and contains no information about you. Results are cached in server memory to avoid repeat lookups.

4. Spotify and other music services

Track Night does not connect to Spotify or any other music service, and never asks you to log in to one. Spotify links you paste into a quiz are saved as plain text with the quiz so the app can give them back to you as a playlist. If you open a playlist file (for example one you exported at exportify.net, a separate service with its own privacy policy), it is read in your browser and only the song names, years and links are saved with your quiz. Whatever you use to play the music, that service's own privacy policy applies.

5. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

6. Children

Track Night is a party game for all ages, but it isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Since there are no accounts, a player only gives a nickname, which is deleted when the game ends.

7. Your rights

Because we don't keep accounts or store personal information after a game ends, there is usually nothing about you for us to access or delete. You control the data on your device through your browser settings. If you are in the EU, UK or another place with data protection law and have a question or request, email us and we'll help.

8. Changes to this policy

If Track Night changes what it collects, for example when the iPhone app, accounts or syncing between devices are added, we will update this policy before that release and change the date at the top.
