Set up a quiz night, step by step

You need a computer to set it up, a TV or laptop on the night, and a phone per team.

1. Make a Spotify playlist

Put the quiz songs in one Spotify playlist. The order doesn't matter yet: you can sort them into categories in Track Night.

2. Export it with names (optional, recommended)

Go to exportify.net, log in with Spotify there and press Export next to your playlist. A CSV file downloads with every song's artist, title and year.

Spotify doesn't let quiz apps read song names, so this file is how they get into Track Night. Exportify is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

3. Start a quiz

On a computer, open Track Night, press Host a quiz, then New quiz. Give the first category a name, like "80s".

4. Add all the songs

Press Add a Spotify playlist, then Open playlist CSV and pick the file from step 2. Every song comes in with artist, title, year and its Spotify link, each with an empty question.

No CSV? In the Spotify desktop app, open the playlist, click a song, press Ctrl/Cmd+A then Ctrl/Cmd+C, and paste in the box. Songs come in without names; open the CSV later and the names are filled in.

5. Sort songs into categories

Tick the songs that belong somewhere else (Select all ticks a category, Shift-click ticks a range), then pick a category, or + New category, under Move to… in the bottom bar. Name each new category. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to change the order within a category.

6. Write the questions

Open a song. Tap a ready-made question (Who sings this?, What year is it from?, Which TV show is this from? …) or press + Add a question, pick its format (text, number, year, multiple choice, artist or song title) and type the question.

Type the right answer. Artist, title and year are already filled in from the CSV, so check them and correct anything that's off. For multiple choice, write the options and tick the right one. Write Top Gun / Topgun to accept either.

Trivia without a song: ? Add a question, no song. A category can be double or triple points or have a countdown.

7. Fix what's missing, then practise

The bar at the top lists anything still missing. When it says Ready to host, press Practice alone: the TV screen and a player's phone side by side, so you can play it through once.

8. Get the music in quiz order

Songs play from your own Spotify, in the quiz's order with shuffle off. If you moved songs around, press Copy Spotify links under Your Spotify playlist and paste them into a new, empty playlist in the Spotify desktop app.

9. Host it on the TV

Press Host this quiz and Full screen for TV. Players scan the QR code (or go to the site and type the 4-letter code). One player per team taps New team; teammates scan the team code on that phone and only type their name.

10. Play

Press Start the quiz. The screen says which song to play: press play in Spotify. Teams answer on their phones, the tiles turn green as they do, and you press Next. Categories follow each other without a break.

11. Check the answers and crown the winner

After the last question every answer is checked at once. Decide the close ones (or let teams mark each other, if you chose that), change any mark, then Show results and Show the winner. Everyone sees their answers next to the right ones.

12. Keep it, share it

Your quiz stays in your browser. Share link sends it to a friend; Save quiz file keeps a backup. Random quiz builds a new night from songs in your saved quizzes.
